Lorem ipsum is the filler print shops have used since the sixteenth century, when
an unknown printer scrambled a passage of Cicero to make a type specimen. The
words mean nothing; the *shape* of the text does. A page of lorem ipsum has the
rhythm, the line lengths and the ragged right edge of real prose, so a layout can
be judged before the copy exists.

## Why a build engine ships one

A book builder is easy to demonstrate with a book that says nothing at all:

- it has chapters, so the table of contents and the running heads have work to do;
- it has headings and links, so the linter has something to check;
- it has no facts, so nothing in it can go stale.
